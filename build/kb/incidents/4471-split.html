The three bugs

  • parser: the shared parser reads dates month first (ticket). Our tenants write the day first, so day and month swap. It covers app and email reports from every tenant, and form reports from booking-v2 tenants, whose hint already says DD/MM/YYYY.
  • hint: booking-v3 asks for month first and reads day first (ticket). The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads the tenant’s way. These are form reports from booking-v3 tenants only. The parser fix does not touch them.
  • shift: the date moves by one day (ticket). Day and month are not swapped. Lisbon gets Tuesday and Toronto gets Monday, “only June” is off, and an invite arrives before the booking. That is a time-zone or daylight-saving bug. The desk already noted these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). Until now the table filed them under parser by tenant.

How the split was made

  • One id, one report: the export pages overlap, so repeated rows are dropped first.
  • The cause comes from the tenant (its locale and booking form, locales.ts), never from intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser. The tagged as column shows how often that guess is wrong.
  • A report the desk read as a one-day move is shift, whatever its tenant.

For the desk

  • Triage by subject (decision 0001) merged all three bugs because the reports share subject lines. Before you merge into a big ticket, check the cause with the dedupe explorer.
  • New date reports: file hint under the booking-v3 ticket, shift under the time-zone ticket, and the rest under #4471.
